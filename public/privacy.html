Waitlist privacy notice

Updated October 29, 2026. This notice covers this landing page and its waitlist, not the future Trifold app.

Who operates this page

Daniela Garza. Contact: dany.gza@gmail.com.

What the waitlist collects

We collect the email address you submit to send early-access and launch updates. This page does not collect your expenses, income, bank account details, or payment information. Signing up does not guarantee access, a launch date, pricing, or a discount.

Services used

Netlify hosts the website and server functions. Resend stores waitlist contacts and processes emails. The operator receives an internal alert when a new contact is created; the alert does not include your email address. Hosting and email services process technical information such as IP addresses and delivery logs to operate and protect these services.

Optional analytics

PostHog loads only if you choose Allow analytics. It measures page visits and signup outcomes. We exclude signup email addresses, form contents, URL query strings, and referrers from analytics events. Session recording is disabled. PostHog still receives network information such as your IP address. Analytics identity stays in memory for the visit. We save only your analytics preference in your browser. You can change your choice at the bottom of the home page. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected.

Operational errors

The site reports fixed error categories and service-health outcomes to detect failures. These reports do not include your email address, form values, or error-message contents. Provider logs and internal delivery alerts help us investigate failures.

Storage and removal

Waitlist contacts are kept to manage early-access updates. We handle removal and deletion requests manually. Email us from the signup address using the Leave the waitlist instructions. Technical logs may remain subject to the providers’ retention settings. This notice does not promise automated removal from third-party backups.

Your choices

You can use the signup form without accepting optional analytics. You can request access, correction, or removal of your waitlist information by contacting us. Future marketing updates must include an unsubscribe option.
